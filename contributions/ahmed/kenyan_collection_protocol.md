# Kenyan Field Data Collection — Protocol 
- Site: Rare Gem Talent School (dyslexia support school).
- Consent: teaacher consent obtained; samples anonymized (no names/faces).
- Capture: flat page, camera above, daylight, crop to written area, 7 pages/student.
- Labeling: 5-point rubric (legibility, baseline, spacing, formation, strokes),
  0-3 = Low Potential, 4-10 = Potential; two raters, tie-break by therapist.
- Metadata: student_id, school_type, status (active/improving/typical), page_date.
- Status: ~290 dysgraphia/improving books collected; typical controls from
  mainstream school pending.
